Live music in Kyoto

Kyoto is better known for temples than for gigs, but it has a small and long-running independent music scene with close ties to the city's universities. Venues are fewer and more intimate than those in Tokyo or Osaka, which makes a Kyoto show a different kind of evening out.

Kyoto's buses and trains finish earlier than you might expect in a city this popular. If you're staying outside the centre, check your route home before the show, or consider a late-night taxi.
