Shimokitazawa SHELTER

下北沢SHELTER

A basement live house in Shimokitazawa and part of the Loft group. It's a small, close-up room focused mainly on rock and punk bands, so expect to be near the stage wherever you stand.

  • Area: Shimokitazawa, Tokyo
  • Nearest station: Shimokitazawa Stn.
