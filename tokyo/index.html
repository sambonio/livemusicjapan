Live music in Tokyo

Tokyo has one of the densest live music scenes in the world, spread across neighbourhoods with very different characters. Shimokitazawa and Koenji are known for small rock and underground venues, Shibuya for mid-sized rooms that host touring bands and rising acts, and Shinjuku for both punk history and serious jazz.

Many venues are a short walk from a major station, but basement entrances can be easy to miss. Allow extra time and check the map before you leave, especially in Shibuya, where station exits can be a long way apart.
