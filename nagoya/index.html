Live music in Nagoya

Nagoya is a regular stop on national tours between Tokyo and Osaka, so it can be a good place to see a band when tickets elsewhere have sold out. Its central Sakae and Yabacho area has shopping, restaurants and live venues close together.

Nagoya is well connected by Shinkansen, but it's worth checking the last train if you're planning a day trip. Most shows finish in time for a late train, though not always in time for a long connection.
