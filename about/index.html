About Live Music Japan

Live Music Japan brings gig schedules from independent venues together in one place, in Japanese and English, for people who live in Japan and people visiting. Japan's live music scene is huge, but it's spread across hundreds of venue websites, most of them only in Japanese. This site aims to make it easier to find a show on any given night.

Where the listings come from

Listings are collected once a day from the public schedule pages of each venue, and every gig links back to the venue's own page. We currently cover 16 venues across Tokyo, Osaka, Kyoto, Nagoya. Band names and event titles appear as the venue wrote them, usually in Japanese.

Listings can be out of date or incomplete, and the venue's own page is always the final word. Please check it before you travel, especially for prices, door times and cancellations.
