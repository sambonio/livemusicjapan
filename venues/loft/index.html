Shinjuku LOFT

新宿LOFT

One of the best-known names in Japanese rock. Loft began in Shinjuku in the 1970s and now runs from Kabukicho, in a basement venue that has hosted generations of punk and rock bands.

  • Area: Shinjuku, Tokyo
  • Nearest station: Seibu-Shinjuku Stn.
