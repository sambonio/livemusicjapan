Shinjuku PIT INN

新宿PIT INN

One of Japan's most respected jazz clubs, in business in Shinjuku since the 1960s. Many schedules have separate afternoon and evening sessions, and it publishes some information in English, which makes it an easy first choice for visitors.

  • Area: Shinjuku, Tokyo
  • Nearest station: Shinjuku-sanchome Stn.
