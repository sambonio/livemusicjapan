Daikanyama UNIT

代官山UNIT

A basement venue in Daikanyama that hosts both live bands and club nights. The surrounding streets are quieter than nearby Shibuya, and it's an easy walk from Daikanyama Station.

  • Nearest station: Daikanyama Stn.
