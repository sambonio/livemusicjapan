Live music in Osaka

Osaka's live music is centred on the south of the city. Shinsaibashi and the nearby Amerikamura area have a cluster of live houses within walking distance of each other, while Horie has smaller independent spaces. Umeda, to the north, is home to larger rooms close to the main rail hub.

Shinsaibashi is a lively area late into the evening, and it can be busy around show times. If you're staying near Namba or Umeda, the Midosuji subway line connects both to Shinsaibashi directly.
