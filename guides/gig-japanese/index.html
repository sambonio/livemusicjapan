Japanese Words and Phrases for Going to a Gig

Most Japanese venue websites are only in Japanese, but schedules follow a very regular pattern. Learn these few terms and you can read nearly any listing. Browser translation handles the rest, though it often garbles band names.

Useful phrases at the venue

  • 当日券はありますか？
    tōjitsuken wa arimasu ka?
    Do you have tickets for today?
  • ドリンク代はいくらですか？
    dorinku-dai wa ikura desu ka?
    How much is the drink charge?
  • ロッカーはありますか？
    rokkā wa arimasu ka?
    Are there lockers?
  • 写真を撮ってもいいですか？
    shashin o totte mo ii desu ka?
    May I take photos?
  • 整理番号は何番まで呼びましたか？
    seiri bangō wa nanban made yobimashita ka?
    What number have they called up to?
  • 最高でした！
    saikō deshita!
    That was amazing! (nice to say at the merch table)

Reading a band's name

Band names are often written in Japanese with no official English version, and the same act may appear in more than one script. That's why our listings show the name as the venue wrote it. If you want to hear a band before you go, copying the exact Japanese text into YouTube or a streaming app usually works best.
