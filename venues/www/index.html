Shibuya WWW

渋谷WWW

A mid-sized venue in Shibuya's Udagawacho district, built in a converted cinema. Its stepped floor helps with sightlines, and the schedule covers indie, electronic and experimental acts as well as touring artists.

  • Area: Shibuya, Tokyo
  • Nearest station: Shibuya Stn.
